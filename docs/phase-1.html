09f719322:42 IST

Add the Heist Director: a request in words becomes a verified level, streamed round by round

Input

you asked me to write the Director's server side while the loader was being built.

Output

POST /api/ai/director streams events (round, spec, verify, done). Each round asks Muse Spark for a level in the schema, runs the verifier, and sends the failures back as the next message, up to four rounds. If the model is down or filters the request, a verified fallback level for the right place is returned (one per place, each checked by a test). Finished requests are cached for an hour; a filtered call is retried once. 25 new tests, 945 in all, type-check and lint clean. Live, with your key:

    • "A night job at a museum: two guards, one dog, steal a painting from the vault." Passed in round 1, 3.0 s: a 10 x 7 museum, vault contract, 2 guards, 1 dog, the lights-out twist.
    • "Quiet refinery sabotage, no dogs, small floor." Failed the exit-distance check twice (6 x 5, then 10 x 5), then passed on round 3 (12 x 7), 6.6 s. The model changed only what the checker named.
    • "A huge bank robbery with ten guards, five dogs, lasers everywhere and a lockdown." Meta's content filter rejected it on both tries (it passed on other tries and with a shorter system prompt, so the filter is borderline), and the fallback level, "The branch vault", came back, with the reason request_rejected.

In plain terms

you can now type what level you want, watch the AI build it, watch the checker say "the exit is too close", and watch the AI fix it. The second example is the demo moment for "verifiable agent". The third shows the safety net.
